# I built 53 AI Agents for 53 VCs to pitch my startup at SF Demo Day by Character Labs

Sean pitches AutoManus at SF Demo Day by Character Labs. AutoManus is "an AI sales agent team for consultative businesses" [00:22]. His background: AI and operations research at MIT, leading product analytics in business messaging at Google (scaled to more than 10 million businesses), and AI product management in merchandising at Walmart (scaled to more than 600 stores). He also runs social channels with about 1 million organic views and 45,000 followers, and his open source projects have about 1,400 GitHub stars, which drives inbound customers.

The pitch contrasts standardized sales (buying an Apple Watch in 3 minutes) with consultative sales, where "you design a tailor solution," taking weeks to months of pre-discovery conversation. Target customers include creative studios, insurance agencies, film production agencies, and architecture firms. Sean argues the space is broken: "60% are dropped because of missed follow-ups. 72% are just wasting time on sales admin" [02:45], and most consultative businesses don't use CRMs because they "creates more admin instead of leverage" [02:37]. He sizes the market as a $30 billion opportunity based on more than 2 million consultative sellers in the US with $15,000 annual contracts [02:54].

AutoManus turns messy conversations into "AI deal memory": launching an agent with zero friction using just a company website and an email, on MCP server or WhatsApp, capturing requirements 24/7 and multi-channel, then surfacing structured leads and value breakdowns each morning. As the demo-day gimmick, "I created 53 agents for every VC" [03:23], and most VCs in the room should have received a link on LinkedIn or email. Traction so far: 125 sign-ups with zero CAC from organic content, seven opportunities in the pipeline, and three main customers at $20,000 ARR. He is raising $3 million to grow the team, improve the product, and hit a $1 million revenue goal.
