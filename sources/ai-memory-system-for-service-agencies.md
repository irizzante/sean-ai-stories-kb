# We Built an AI Memory System for Your Service Agencies, Inspired by Claude

The video announces an AI product pivot for customers that are "either service business or a consultative agency or creative studios" [00:13], whose number one pain point is "what's it going on in my business? How's it going with that client? And is that person going to close or are they going to churn?" [00:17]. CRMs were supposed to solve this, but "70 to 80% of them have never used a CRM tool", tracking deals instead in communication apps or a Google Sheet [00:30]. The deeper diagnosis is that the problem was never documenting data: "it's so easy to have data in one place, but those data will decay over time" [00:52]. What these businesses actually want is a memory system that acts as "an internal AI chief of staff or like having a Jarvis for your business" [00:59].

The build was inspired by Claude Code: the team runs their entire startup on Notion, GitHub, Linear, Slack, and Claude Code, and the founder can simply turn it on and ask "what is going on in my startup" [02:00]. That experience became the Business Manager, a right-hand sidebar inside the Leads Hub that lets an owner ask about a lead, see conversation history, and act in the same flow [02:09]. The demo follows a high-value lead named Lucy: the manager recalls the previous conversation - a "see you on Monday kickoff at 10:00 a.m." that never happened - then drafts and sends a follow-up via WhatsApp to rebook for Tuesday, all from the chat [02:19]. The WhatsApp integration runs through the product's AI features "where you can train your own agents" [03:06].

Because some sizable customers do use a CRM, the system also syncs external data: HubSpot is connected, and leads like Calvin and Palmar are pulled in so they can be enabled "with our memory system and the business manager" [03:37]. The product is positioned as an extension of the earlier WhatsApp messaging leads management tool, with the team now "all in on building the memory system and a second brain for the business owners in consultative agencies and service industries" [04:11].
