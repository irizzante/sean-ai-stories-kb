# Jason Zhou

Jason Zhou is AI Jason: "His real name is Jason Zhou. Jason is based in Sydney, Australia. He used to be a product designer and then he turned into AI builder, startup founder, and currently he founded superdesign.dev and... a new product called trulyt.io" [01:37], with 222,000 YouTube subscribers, 30.5k on X, and a 1,281-member AI Builder Club [01:34].

His path is a chain of pivots. Originally from Guangzhou [02:33], he studied power engineering in Chongqing wanting nuclear power - "unfortunately, back then, China Chinese university would mainly teach you how to burn coal" [02:52] - then became a product designer, moved to Sydney for his girlfriend [03:09], and joined SafetyCulture, "a small startup... it did turn out to be one of the unicorn in Australia" that he watched grow "from 50 people to later 700" [03:27]. His first startup came from "one of my hackathon project got funded by Binance" [04:18], and it failed after a year. The GPT-3.5 API hooked him [04:31], and he started a YouTube channel to maintain learning momentum [05:09]; his first video went up May 4, 2023 [05:33], with an earlier Kanye West AI-voice-clone experiment in Google Colab [05:44].

His signature is the Jian-Yang hook - "I put Jian Yang's face on the thumbnail" [00:56] - and prototype-first teaching: "I basically try new technology understand the concept build some prototype" [06:45] so viewers "not only understand concept but also know what they can do about it" [06:51]. Sean learned LangChain and LangGraph from his videos [07:00]. His products extend the style: Superdesign (first Supernova) began as a weekend Cursor extension with an infinite canvas, "almost like Figma inside IDE" [16:22], hit 4,000 GitHub stars [16:26], and he "went to US, did our first round" [16:44]. At Relevance AI he "locked my design team in a room for a day" to design inside the codebase [20:03]; they began shipping three to five PRs a week [20:38]. Trulyt grew from OpenClaw experiments automating support, growth, and ops [26:26], after a Cruise friend said "team who use agent analytics is going to be 10x more effective" [27:09]; its launch story includes an agent detecting referral-credit farming, running SQL, and resetting the credits itself [30:28].

His theses: "if you are in AI, every day you will feel like AGI is arrived and your business is going to die. But if you just take a break in the mountain for a month and you come back you realize everything is still the same" [36:41]; long-running self-improving agents with memory are the next wave [35:06]; every company gets its own Jarvis [39:52]; and content boils down to "you basically just need to make useful things" [43:27].
