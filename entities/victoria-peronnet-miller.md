# Victoria Peronnet Miller

Victoria is a 24-year-old British founder introduced in the same episode: "I'm creating ventureship, which is basically hiring reinvented" [01:56]. Instead of AI screenings and psychometric tests, companies convert a problem into a project and "five to 10 individuals give their innovative and creative solutions" [02:19]; companies expand hiring funnels or CSR, and candidates "either get hired or have something in your portfolio" [02:30].

The origin is her friends' experience: "loads of my friends coming out of university, they couldn't get a job because of all these like AI screenings, psychometric testings... applications were kind of going into a bit of a void" [06:29]. She catalogued "four problems" [06:50] - jobseekers locked out, bad hires from AI-gated hiring, hiring time and cost, and all-consuming wrong hires. She studied Chinese and business at Durham [41:03] and went through the Durham University Venture Lab incubator [50:04] plus grant funding [50:20], where advisors taught her the "migraine versus headache" problem test [42:34]. She practices the model herself: "I was hiring two interns... I practice what I preached and I hired them on like the projects, gave them like a take-home project" [13:29].

Her sharpest lines come from cross-Atlantic experience. Ten days in an SF accelerator left her humbled: "I don't know what they drink over there. They've got rocket fuel in their veins" [18:08]; "America's more in conviction rather than proof and the UK's more in proof because we're so risk averse" [19:06]; and the UK "want both" - founder belief and proof - which is "so much more bureaucratic" [22:16]. On perfectionism: "being messy is actually such an advantage cuz it means that you put yourself out there before you feel ready" [62:58]. Her worst networking memory - a man putting his hand in her face and saying "Stop" [51:11] - became her lesson: "just question why" [53:11].

She is building a side venture with Maya called Two and Through [45:44] with a segment called "to cake or not to cake" - delivering actual cakes to cold-call investors and companies [46:25]. Find her at Victoria Peronnet Miller on LinkedIn, ventureship.co.uk, and TikTok/Instagram as Who Knows HQ [64:54].
