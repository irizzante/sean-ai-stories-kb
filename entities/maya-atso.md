# Maya Atso

Maya is a 24-year-old British founder who met Sean on TikTok [00:36] and used the episode to unveil her company: "I'm building a company called Rebeauty. Rebeauty is essentially Vinted, but for beauty" [01:33] - a marketplace for reselling unopened, unused beauty products, "whether that be PR, unwanted gifts, miss returns" [01:50].

Her thesis is a taxonomy argument. Fashion marketplaces host beauty listings but are "built for fashion" [04:47]: "if you're searching for a serum, you'd search for ingredients that you'd like, like vitamin C, retinol, hyaluronic acid, niacinamide" [04:24], plus morning/night usage and skin concerns like acne or rosacea. AI-personalized search is "definitely in our future plans... cuz it's the way forward in shopping, isn't it? AI commerce" [05:46]. She found the market as a customer: during lockdown she obsessed over Dr. Barbara Sturm's £200 creams [35:46], could not afford them, and discovered beauty listings on Depop, Vinted, and eBay [36:17]. Her first buyer "walked away and then went and grabbed her makeup bag and plonked it in front of the camera... 'Every single product you see here, I've purchased on Vinted'" [37:00], and she tested demand by working Brick Lane: "spoke to hundreds of people that day, gave out free samples" [40:11].

On working style she is AI-positive but human-first: "AI is amazing. It's actually my best friend in growing this startup" [12:41], yet she plans a part-time social media hire because AI "can't actually create that content that is authentic" [13:18]. She is looking for a CTO - "I need to find someone technical that can help in areas that I am not good at" [11:13] - and repeats her dad's mantra: "the difference between a winner or a loser is a winner never gives up" [29:52]. She cites Canva's 100 VC rejections [49:26] and Odd Muse founder Amy's push-through point [30:03] as motivation, and warns against comparison: "comparison is completely the thief of joy" [33:17].

She can be found at Instagram and TikTok re:beauty.app, LinkedIn re:beauty, her personal LinkedIn Maya Atso, and www.rebeauty.app [65:10].
