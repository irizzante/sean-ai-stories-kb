# Andrew Chen

Andrew Chen appears in the corpus as "the partner at A16Z" who personally "messaged me on Twitter on X" [00:40] after Sean posted that he had received three a16z speedrun invitation emails, including one claiming he was "at the top 5% of their cohort" [00:19]. Chen was joined by "their marketing partner Ryan" [00:46].

What Chen did is the point of the video. Rather than ignoring a public post, "basically they're just trying to find out what's the reason and they asked me about what emails I had" [00:49], which surfaced the mundane explanation: Sean had applied with one or two addresses and also signed up to the a16z Substack with a couple of emails [00:59]. "So immediately they jump into the debugging mode" [01:10].

Sean's judgment elevates this to a norm: "Imagine partners at a fund like A16Z just messaging you and trying to debug with you together. I think this is true founder mode" [01:16], evidence that "these days VCs behave like founders too" [01:23] - and a model for founders engaging public customer feedback [01:31]. Chen's outreach sits alongside the fact that "some people commented on my tweet saying that they also got spammed" [01:52], a caveat Sean acknowledges while crediting the overall effort to "acquiring the best founders in the world to apply" [01:44].
