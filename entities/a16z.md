# Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)

In this video, a16z appears through its speedrun program's outreach machinery. "Today the partners at A16Z speedrun sent out the invitations to apply to the latest cohort and I received uh three of them" [00:00]. The reason was mundane - Sean had applied with one or two emails and "also signed up to their Substack mail list with a couple of emails" [00:59] - but one of the three said he was "at the top 5% of their cohort so they invited us to apply with our company" [00:19], which Sean joked he would use to apply.

The defining moment is the partners' response to his X post. "The partner at A16Z Andrew Chen messaged me on Twitter on X. Their marketing partner Ryan also messaged me" [00:40], and "immediately they jump into the debugging mode" [01:10]. Sean's read: "Imagine partners at a fund like A16Z just messaging you and and trying to debug with you together. I think this is uh true founder mode" [01:16].

The takeaway he draws is organizational: "it's really interesting that these days VCs behave like founders too" [01:23], and they model what founders should do - "when your potential customers or existing customers are talking about your products publicly you should read them and engage with your customers" [01:31]. He credits them with pushing "really hard on acquiring the best founders in the world to apply" [01:41], even as commenters on his tweet reported being spammed [01:52]. Either way, "they're doing a good job in terms of getting close to their customers and getting to know their customers" [02:00].
