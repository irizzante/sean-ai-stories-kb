# AutoManus

AutoManus is Sean Chen's startup: "We're an AI sales agent team for consultative businesses" [00:22]. The name predates the Manus wave - "when we started this company Manus wasn't popular yet. We were called Automanus. Then they blew up" [02:12] - and draws on the MIT motto: "the word 'Manus' in MIT's motto, it means 'hand.' Mind and hand working together" [02:17]. (The demo-day transcript renders it "Automannus" [00:06].)

The pitch defines its market by contrast with standardized sales: an Apple Watch is searched, clicked, and checked out in three minutes [01:21], while consultative sales mean "you design a tailor solution. You take weeks to months for the pre-discovery conversation" [01:33] - creative studios, insurance agencies, film production, architecture firms [01:45]. The product turns "these messy conversations into AI deal memory" [03:05]: an agent launched with "just a company website and an email" [03:25], available "on our MCV server or WhatsApp" [03:30], capturing requirements 24/7 so a founder wakes up to "20 leads lying down there, all structured for you" [04:01]. Traction at the SF Demo Day: "125 sign-ups from businesses with zero CAC because of my organic content. Seven ops in the pipeline and three main customers with $20,000 ARR" [04:33], raising "$3 million to grow our team, improve our product, and hit the revenue goal" [04:52].

The stunt that frames it all: for the demo day, "I created 53 agents for every VC" [03:19], each sent a personalized link on LinkedIn or email. It appears again in the AI Jason podcast, where Sean describes "we're building Automaton [AutoManus], which is helping long cycle sales teams to manage their sales memories" [38:02], including an agent deciding whether a stalled deal "is still a warm deal or is it already cold" [38:50]. The same episode previews the MCP-server angle covered in the [MCP in AI Agents](/concepts/mcp-in-ai-agents.md) cluster.
